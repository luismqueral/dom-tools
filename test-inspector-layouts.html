Drag My Edges

Hover near any edge to see the green handle. Drag to resize padding — snaps to design tokens.

Pro: Most intuitive — works like resizing in Figma. Immediate spatial feedback. No panel needed for quick tweaks. Con: Small hit target (edge handles). Needs clear affordance. Only works for spacing, not color/type.

Scroll On Me

Hover here and use the scroll wheel to cycle through spacing tokens. Hold Shift for margin, Alt for font-size.

Pro: Zero clicks — fastest possible interaction. Familiar from Figma number inputs. Keeps hands in position. Con: Fights page scroll — needs clear activation (maybe only when element is "selected"). Discoverability is low.
